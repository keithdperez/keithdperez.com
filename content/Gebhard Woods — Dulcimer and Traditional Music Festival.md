## Notes

- Monitor cables are already attached, and you can find the 1/4 cables in the back of the mixer rack.
- Camera will need power cable + 2 XLR cables for audio. Please connect the XLR cables to **Aux 3 & 4**, on the bottom channels of the mixer.
- Connect the Shure shotgun mic into **Ch. 16**, and you can Gaff tape the mic onto the top of the mixer rack, pointing to the crowd.
- Make sure to turn on the camera, and that you see green audio signal coming into the camera after plugging in. There should be 2 SD Cards in the camera already. Best practice: do a quick record test.

- If you need more overall monitor volume, you can turn up the dials on the monitor amp, underneath the mixer. *(it should be pretty set where it's at though)*
