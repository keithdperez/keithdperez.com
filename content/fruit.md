# 🍂 The Fruit of Sin vs. 🌿 The Fruit of the Spirit


A biblical contrast between life led by the flesh and life led by the Spirit.

---

## 🍂 The Fruit of Sin

**Root:** Living according to the flesh — self-centeredness, pride, and rebellion against God.  

**Scripture:** *Galatians 5:19–21; Romans 6:21–23*

**Produces:**

- Sexual immorality and impurity  
- Idolatry and selfish ambition  
- Hatred, jealousy, and fits of rage  
- Dissension, envy, drunkenness  

- Greed, deceit, and spiritual death  

  

**➡️ Result:** Spiritual death and separation from God  

> “The wages of sin is death.” — *Romans 6:23*  

  

Sin promises freedom but produces bondage. It corrupts what is good, turning:

- Love into lust  

- Confidence into pride  

- Rest into apathy  

  

---

  

## 🌿 The Fruit of the Spirit

  

**Root:** Living by the Holy Spirit — surrendering to God and being transformed by His presence.  

**Scripture:** *Galatians 5:22–23; John 15:4–5*

  

**Produces:**

- **Love** — selfless and sacrificial  

- **Joy** — deep contentment in God  

- **Peace** — inner rest and reconciliation  

- **Patience** — enduring with grace  

- **Kindness & Goodness** — active compassion  

- **Faithfulness** — steadfast reliability  

- **Gentleness** — strength under control  

- **Self-control** — mastery through the Spirit  

  

**➡️ Result:** Eternal life, freedom, and flourishing  

> “By their fruit you will know them.” — *Matthew 7:16*

  

The Spirit’s fruit shows the evidence of God’s kingdom at work in a person.

  

---

  

## 🌾 Comparison Table

  

| Category | Fruit of Sin | Fruit of the Spirit |

|-----------|---------------|---------------------|

| **Source** | Flesh / Self | Holy Spirit |

| **Nature** | Corrupt, selfish | Holy, life-giving |

| **Produces** | Bondage, guilt, death | Freedom, joy, peace |

| **Focus** | Pleasing self | Reflecting Christ |

| **End Result** | Separation from God | Eternal life and unity with God |

  

---

  

> “For the mind governed by the flesh is death, but the mind governed by the Spirit is life and peace.”  

> — *Romans 8:6*
