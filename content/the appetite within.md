# You are starving.

> As the deer panteth for the water, so my soul longeth after thee.

It is in man’s soul that the corruption of this life comes. God said we can eat of any tree in the garden of Eden. We could feed as we pleased, but we took from what was not given to us. We wanted more. Our appetite for the best is us.

What is it about discipline and pain and restraint? It seems like there’s something to choosing “no” that produces a better result. Tearing your muscles makes them grow. Something about crushing the grapes to create the wine. Something about pressing the olives until the oil is given to be consumed.

Jesus blood was the oil needed for our trespasses.

“The human appetite turns destructive the moment it bleeds past what God gives. Not because desire is bad, but because desire without boundary becomes parasitic.”

“You think you’re feasting, but you’re starving. And the proof is that you can’t stop eating.”
