# A Challenge from the Lord

**Stop outsourcing your faith.**  
You don’t need to wait for permission, or for someone to validate what’s already burning in you. You’ve been handed a voice, a vision, and a burden that keeps resurfacing — and you’ve treated it like an optional side project instead of the main thing.

## The Challenge

**Take ownership of what I’ve already spoken.**  
Stop “preparing” and start *embodying*.  
Stop studying rest and actually *rest*.  
Stop talking about leading and *lead*.  
Stop praying for clarity when you already know the next faithful step — take it, and let Me meet you there.

It’s less about doing more, and more about *standing up* in what’s already been given.

---

### A Daily Practice

Ask yourself each morning:  
> *What truth have I been avoiding living out?*

Then make **one small move** that proves you believe it.
