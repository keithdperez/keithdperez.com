# Mix Chain Ideas (SSL 4K B Workflow)

## Drums (heavy, indie/rock punch)
1. **SSL 4K B** – drive input for punch, EQ for snare crack (3–5k), tame boxiness (~300Hz), light comp (2–3dB GR).  
2. **API 2500 (UAD)** – bus glue if needed (fast attack, medium release).  
3. **Valhalla Room** – light ambience for space, or **Supermassive** for indie textures.  
- *Optional:* **RC-20** or **Decapitator** for grit/noise.

---

## Bass
1. **SSL 4K B** – tighten low mids, light comp to control peaks.  
2. **UAD LA-2A (Gray)** – smooth, musical compression.  
3. **Studer A800** – low-end weight, subtle tape saturation.  

---

## Guitars (droning / textured layers)
1. **SSL 4K B** – roll out mud (~200–300Hz), push presence (2–3k), input drive for grit.  
2. **FabFilter Pro-Q 4** – surgical cleanup of resonances.  
3. **Valhalla VintageVerb / Supermassive** – depth, trails, indie texture.  
- *Optional:* **RC-20** or **SketchCassette** for lo-fi edge.

---

## Keys / Pads (Keyscape textures)
1. **SSL 4K B** – subtle comp, add high-shelf for air.  
2. **Pro-Q 4** – tame harshness (2–4k).  
3. **Valhalla Shimmer / Supermassive** – widen space, create atmosphere.  

---

## Vocals
1. **SSL 4K B** – broad EQ (remove mud, add top sparkle), light comp (2–4dB).  
2. **UAD 1176** – fast comp to catch peaks.  
3. **Nectar 4** – de-ess and shaping.  
4. **Valhalla VintageVerb** – plate or chamber reverb for indie vibe.  
- *Optional:* **Decapitator** for grit, **Vocal Doubler** for width.

---

## Mix Bus (glue + polish)
1. **SSL Bus Comp (UAD)** – 1–2dB GR for glue.  
2. **Ampex ATR-102** – light tape for depth and cohesion.  
3. **Ozone 11 Maximizer** – limiter stage (or **Pro-L2** later for transparency).  
4. **Insight 2** – metering (LUFS, true peak).  

---

## Workflow Mindset
- **4K B = vibe shaper.** Use first on each channel for tone, drive, and quick EQ.  
- **FabFilter / UAD = precision.** Only step in when 4K B isn’t enough.  
- **Valhalla / RC-20 = atmosphere.** Sprinkle in texture and space.  
- **Bus = cohesion.** Gentle compression, subtle tape, final limiter.
