This page is for when I lose my way, or start bending toward who others want me to be.
It’s not a brand. It’s not a script.
It’s my **why**. My center. My anchor.

I’ve lived a lot of life trying to be accepted.
I’ve adapted, observed, stayed out of conflict, read the room before I ever read myself.
But this is what I’m returning to.

## Personal Mission Statement

I exist to show acceptance to the outcasts.
Those shown judgment and hurt will find unconditional love and kindness where they were treated unkind.

**Keith is a man of his word to others, approachable to his family, and has laid down his life.**

---

## My Calling in the Circles of My Life

### FAMILY
- Loving.
- Guards down.
- Approachable, safe.
- You can tell me anything.
- I’m a friend.
- I pull people out of dark into light—and help keep them there.
- I’m an optimist in the middle of storms.

### FRIENDS
- You can go deep with me.
- Loyal.
- Trustworthy.
- You feel heard with me.
- I create space where people feel like they *belong*.

### PROFESSION
- I come through.
- I problem-solve when others freeze.
- I help us move forward.
- I give my tools away.
- I equip people, not just serve them.

### CHURCH
- I help people go from caterpillar to butterfly.
- I help them bloom inside.
- From gray to color.
- I help people become vibrant in God.
- I open doors of opportunity.
- I make the disqualified feel qualified again.

---

## Guiding Truths

- I don’t need to morph into who others expect—*I already carry what’s needed.*
- I bring unconditional love, not to be accepted, but because I *am* accepted.
- I create space for others to be themselves because I’ve learned to be myself.
- I don’t run from conflict—I face things now with love and courage.
- I’m not afraid to be misunderstood. Truth always finds its voice.
- I trust what God put in me. I move with quiet authority, not performance.
- I build from identity, not insecurity.
- I don’t need the room to approve me—I walk in already approved.

---

## Who Do I Want To Be Known For?

Someone who carries light.
Someone who is safe, steady, and honest.
Someone who leads people out of shame and into freedom.
Someone who builds, not just dreams.
Someone who loves like Jesus.
