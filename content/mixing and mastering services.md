## What's Included

**Mixing:**
- Balance and dynamics processing
- EQ, compression, and effects
- Stereo imaging and depth
- Up to 3 revision rounds
- Delivered as high-quality WAV files

**Mastering:**
- Final EQ and compression
- Loudness optimization
- Sequencing and spacing (for multi-track projects)
- Multiple format delivery (WAV, MP3, streaming-optimized)

---

## Process

1. **Send your tracks** — Share stems or multitracks via Google Drive, Dropbox, or WeTransfer
2. **Reference call** — Brief discussion about vision, influences, and any specific needs
3. **First mix/master** — Initial version delivered within 5-7 days (singles) or 2-3 weeks (EPs/LPs)
4. **Revisions** — Up to 3 rounds of adjustments based on your feedback
5. **Final delivery** — All formats delivered, ready for release
