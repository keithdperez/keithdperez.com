# Build an Inner Court

_Don’t just build a life people admire — build a place where God dwells._

You can have ambition, gifts, favor, and even fruit…  
but if you don’t build an **inner court**, it all becomes fragile.

The outer court is where people see you.  
The inner court is where you meet with God.

---

## The Pattern

In the Old Testament, the tabernacle had layers:

- The **outer court** — visible, accessible, busy.  
- The **inner court (Holy Place)** — sacred, reserved, intentional.  
- The **Holy of Holies** — where God’s presence dwelled.

Jesus tore the veil so you could live from that center.  
But most men stay stuck outside — admired, active, empty.

---

## Signs You Haven’t Built an Inner Court

- You process life mostly through productivity or performance  
- You don’t know how to sit in stillness without reaching for a distraction  
- You feel distant from God but don’t know how to draw near  
- You live reactive, rarely reflective  
- You sound wise in public, but feel scattered and dry in private

---

## What the Inner Court Is

- A **spiritual place** — where you and God meet  
- A **mental place** — where thoughts are sorted and stilled  
- An **emotional place** — where you tell the truth before God, not just others  
- A **sacred place** — where you hear the real assignment and let Him shape you

---

## How to Build It

- **Make space before the day makes demands.**  
  Don’t let others dictate your soul’s center.

- **Name what’s crowding it.**  
  Old wounds. Addictions. Noise. Hurry. Shame. Clear it out.

- **Create liturgy.**  
  You don’t need legalism — you need rhythm. Word. Prayer. Stillness. Repentance. Joy.

- **Return often.**  
  Don’t just visit when things get hard. Dwell there. 

> “One thing I ask… that I may dwell in the house of the Lord all the days of my life…”  
> — Psalm 27:4

## Final Word

You can build platforms, companies, churches, relationships —  
but if the inner court is neglected, it all collapses under pressure.

Don’t just work for God.  
Walk with Him.

_What you build in secret is what will carry you in the storm._
