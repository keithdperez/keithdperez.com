# Jam Progression Cheat Sheet

These are loopable, warm-sounding progressions you can use for jamming.  
Written in Nashville numbers so you can transpose on the fly.

---

## A. Sunlit Bossa
| 1maj7  | 6-7b5   | 2m7   | 5-9   |
Example in C: Cmaj7 – Am7b5 – Dm7 – G9

---

## B. Warm & Soulful
| 1maj7  | 3m7     | 6m7   | 2m7  5  |
Example in Bb: Bbmaj7 – Dm7 – Gm7 – Cm7  F

---

## C. Neo-Soul Float
| 1maj7  | 4maj7   | 3m7   | 6m7   |
Example in A: Amaj7 – Dmaj7 – C#m7 – F#m7

---

## D. Feel-Good Pop Groove
| 1maj7  | 5/7     | 6m7   | 4maj7 |
Example in G: Gmaj7 – D/F# – Em7 – Cmaj7

---

## E. Ocean Jam Vamp
| 4maj7  | 5sus4   |
Example in D: Gmaj7 – Asus4

---

### Tips to Keep It Fresh
- Change rhythmic feel (straight 8ths, swung, bossa, halftime)
- Use alternate voicings (drop 2, spread triads, open strings)
- Modulate or move shapes up/down for new colors
- Layer simple percussion loops underneath for groove
