## Using the System

**To play outside only:**
1. In the Sonos app, select **Living Room**
2. Turn down the **left dial** (indoor)
3. Turn up the **right dial** (labeled "Outside") to desired volume

**To play inside only:**
- Select **Living Room** in the app
- Turn up the **left dial**, turn down the **right dial**

**To play in multiple zones:**
- Open the Sonos app
- Tap “Group” and select the zones you want (e.g. Living Room + Portable)

## Troubleshooting

**No sound outside:**
- Confirm music is playing to **Living Room**
- Check that the **"Outside" dial** is turned up
- Ensure app volume is not muted

**No sound from Basement TV:**
- Check TV audio output is set to **HDMI ARC/eARC**
- Restart the TV or Sonos Arc if needed
- Verify volume in the Sonos app

**Speakers missing in app:**
- Open Sonos app > Settings > System
- Check Wi-Fi connection
- Restart router and devices if needed
