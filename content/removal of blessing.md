# When Does God Remove Blessing? 

## 1. Disobedience to God’s Covenant
- **Deuteronomy 28** – Blessings for obedience, curses for disobedience.

## 2. Pride and Unrepentant Sin
- **James 4:6** – God opposes the proud but gives grace to the humble.
- **Psalm 84:11** – No good thing withheld from those who walk uprightly.

## 3. Injustice and Neglect
- **Malachi 3:8–9** – Under a curse for robbing God in tithes.
- **Isaiah 1:15–17** – God refuses prayers when justice is ignored.

## 4. Broken Relationships
- **1 Peter 3:7** – Mistreating your wife hinders your prayers.
- **Matthew 5:23–24** – Reconcile before offering gifts at the altar.

## 5. Seasons of Testing
- **Job** – Loss wasn’t punishment, but testing.
- **John 15:2** – Pruning leads to greater fruitfulness.

---

## Key Distinction
- Blessing is tied to **God’s covenant faithfulness**, not human approval.  
- A spouse cannot revoke God’s blessing.
