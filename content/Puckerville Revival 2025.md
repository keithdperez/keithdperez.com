# Puckerville Revival 2025 Meeting Notes
*Date: 2025-06-09*

---

## Intent and Focus  
The intention behind the songs for the day is to invite God to show up and to encourage others to consider Him. This serves as an open invitation to draw closer to Him today.

> “The song *Consider Him* is what made me want to do this day.”

We will likely close the session with this song if we proceed as planned.

## Musical Arrangement  
For the songs *Rest on Us* and *Great Are You Lord*, we plan to arrange them as a “round” — a musical technique where these songs are sung in tandem, creating a continuous, overlapping harmony.

## Tone  
- Invitational  
- Opening the door for encounter and reflection
