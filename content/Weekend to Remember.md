## Man to Man

Whatever you take into your mind, in your eyes, ears, heart — it gots down into your heart. Whichever dog you feed wins the fight.

**EGO** = Erasing God Out
### Things to begin doing:

- everyday pray for your family
- Appreciate her for the things she does
		“The quickest way to kill romance is to take each other for granted…”

You say “I’d be willing to die for Julianna” — but honestly, you’re not ready to die for her until you’re ready to live for her.

Be honest and trustworthy. 

[[The Gospel According to MATTHEW 6]] — “Seek first the kingdom of God, and everything else shall be added onto you.”

### So what should I be doing?

1. Die to yourself
2. Affirm and step into your wife’s world
3. Show her and tell her you love her
4. Continue pursing her
5. Pray with her
6. Be faithful in every area (guard your eyes!)
7. Never mistreat her in any way

## Love your wife by being a student of her

> "Be good husbands to your wives. Honor them, delight in them. As women they lack some of your advantages. But in the now life of God's grace, you're equals. Treat your wives, then, as equals so your prayers don't run aground."
> — [[The First Epistle of PETER 3|1 Peter 3:7]] (MSG)

1. What does she think about?
2. What does she love? What are her gifts and abilities?
3. What does she dream of accomplishing?
4. How does she feel love?
5. What bothers her?
6. How does she want to resolve conflict?
7. How does she’s want to follow God together with you?

#foodforthought — who I follow is who Julianna will be following… if I follow my own heart, then Julianna will be led to live for me. If I follow God’s heart, then Julianna will be led to live for God.
