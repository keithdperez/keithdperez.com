# LED Wall Proposal for Reconciliation Church

**Prepared by:** Keith Perez
**Date:** October 13, 2025
**Location:** [Reconciliation Church — South Holland, IL](https://maps.apple/p/JjkBbU_6k_bIQj)

---

## Overview

Installation of a **2.9 mm indoor LED wall** for the main sanctuary.  
The wall will sit on the ground, centered beneath the cross art, following the angle of the existing back wall.  
All gear, mounting, wiring, and configuration will be coordinated and installed by then, create.
