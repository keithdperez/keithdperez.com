# Mixing Cheat Sheet

#### Focus:
- width, contrast, emotion, and grit
- Keep drums punchy, guitars layered but clean, vocals wide and expressive
- Prioritize vibe over polish; automate FX and dynamics

---

## Drums (Slate Trigger + Processing)

### Kick
- Trigger: Tight + Sub-heavy sample
- EQ: Cut 300Hz, sub shelf at 50Hz
- Comp: 1176 (fast attack, med release, 4:1)
- Optional: Saturn 2 or RC-20 (parallel) for weight or grit
- Mono-sum sub region with Ozone Imager if needed

### Snare
- Trigger: Blend snappy + body samples
- Comp: Distressor or API 2500 (4:1, medium-fast)
- EQ: Notch 2.5–4k harshness
- Verb: Valhalla Plate (1.4s, predelay ~20ms)
- Optional: SketchCassette or RC-20 for lo-fi lift (parallel)

### Toms
- Trigger and fade tails
- Gate > EQ > Comp
- Tune to key of song

### Drum Buss
- API Vision or SSL-style comp (slow attack, med release)
- EQ: Low cut, boost 5k for presence
- Optional: RC-20 or Saturn on parallel for dirt
- Final cleanup with Pro-Q 4 if needed

---

## Guitars

### Processing
- Dual-panned rhythm tracks
- EQ: HP @ 90Hz, LP @ 10kHz, scoop 300–400Hz mud
- Buss Comp: Distressor or API Vision for glue
- Optional: Saturn 2 on low-mids
- Room Verb: Valhalla Room (short decay, low mix)
- Imager: Widen bussed guitars subtly if needed

---

## Synths & Pads

- EQ: HP @ 120Hz+, notch resonances
- FX: RC-20, Supermassive, FilterShaper XL for movement
- Imager: Widen ambient layers, keep melodic parts centered
- Optional: Dynamic EQ to duck during vocal moments

---

## Lead Vocals (Tyler)

### Chain
- Preamp: Avalon VT-737 or Nectar input EQ
- Comp 1: LA-2A Gray (slow glue)
- Comp 2: Neutron or 1176 (fast peak control)
- EQ: Clean 300–500Hz, bright shelf, notch 2k if harsh
- Optional: Saturn 2 (parallel) for texture or density

### FX
- Plate Verb: Valhalla Plate or Pure Plate
- Slap Delay: Mono, ~90ms, low-pass around 5k
- Throw Delay: Galaxy or FreqEcho on automation
- Modulation: Nectar Dimension or Chorus (light)
- Saturation FX: RC-20 or VocalSynth 2 (for stylized moments)

---

## Vocal Stacks / Harmonies

- Hard L/R panning (offset timing slightly)
- EQ: Cut lows, brighten upper mids
- Imager: Use on group bus if needed
- Reverb: Longer, darker plate
- Optional: Bus into subtle FreqEcho or RC-20 for width or lo-fi grit

---

## Bass

- EQ: HP @ 30Hz, cut 250Hz if muddy
- Comp: API 2500 or Neutron (tight, punchy)
- Saturation: Saturn 2 or RC-20 (midrange lift)
- Tighten with multiband or transient shaping if needed
- Keep mono below 150Hz

---

## Master Buss

- EQ: Pro-Q 4 for tonal balance
- Glue Comp: API 2500 or VariMu (1–2 dB gain reduction)
- Tape: Studer A800 or Ozone Vintage Tape
- Limiter: Ozone Maximizer, low transient emphasis
- Reference: Use Tonal Balance Control to compare

---

## FX & Utility Busses

- Slap Delay: Mono, ~80–100ms
- Plate Reverb: Short-to-medium decay, low mix
- Throw Delay: Galaxy Echo, automated per phrase
- Lo-fi Bus: SketchCassette or RC-20 (drums, vocal throw moments)
- Parallel Saturation: Saturn 2 or Distressor for extra weight
- Width Bus: Modulated reverb + Ozone Imager (guitars, pads)

---

## Reminders

- Print all triggers before mixing
- Set initial buss compression early
- Automate reverb/delay sends, don’t rely on static levels
- Keep checking against rough mix for emotional compass
- Build in contrast: dry vs washed, intimate vs wide, clean vs broken
