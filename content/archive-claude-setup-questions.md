## 2. TYPICAL WORKFLOWS

### Church Tech - Describe your typical week:

Example: "Monday: plan service, Tuesday: rehearsal prep, Wednesday: team meeting..."

I work based off of requests. my week varies depending on what job requests I get and what events are going on between work, church etc. here's a loose idea though:

Monday: flex day. my wife Julianna goes into church, and I either go there with her and accomplish emails, Ableton projects, CLI stuff, or church based projects.

Tuesday: most open day for my work -- especially between 9am-2pm.

Wednesday: Church work day. I maintain the church's AV system, update or create graphics, upgrade or update the church's website thetablechurch.life via Squarespace, help out with The Living Room coffeeshop with any graphics or web stuff they need etc. all off squarespace. I use some adobe stuff as well for that. In the evening, we do worship rehearsal with Sunday's worship team.

Thursday: Men's bible study group in the morning. I start the day reading scripture (NKJV always), and then go to the group. then, the rest of the day is either home maintenance and projects, or else I work events etc. if there were any requests that day.

Friday: Our Sabbath day of rest. If I have events, I'll work those, otherwise I try to spend time with Julianna.

Saturday: Either it's events involving family, church, or travel. I make sure that I input pastor Dave's sermon slides into the church computer, by remoting into the machine (ss table) and getting on ProPresenter and following the google Docs link that he emails me every week. I download a PDF of it, and whatever he marks in RED on his doc is what I add as scripture slides or quotes onto slides in ProPresenter. I also take that time to make sure everything is in order for service on propresenter, like lyrics, background slides, Macros, MIDI cues, LightKey cues for lights etc.

Sunday: church service in the morning. Make sure everything is turned on, computer is logged on, turn on the 3 displays, open propresenter, turn on the stage montitors and the main PA amp. turn on all the instruments, like the Roland v-Drums, Roland RD-88, bass amp, wireless guitar. schedule YouTube live schedule for 10am in youtube studio. ProPresenter has a reoccurring calendar date to start captureat that time. I go into the churhc youtube account and manually schedule a recording, so that when it starts, it finds the recieve from propresenter and starts streaming on youtube. the last slide of the propresenter set has an action to stop capture, which stops streaming. the rest of the day is either family time, or house stuff, prepping for the week.


### Design Business - Describe your typical project flow:

Example: "Client inquiry → discovery call → proposal → design → revisions → delivery..."

FOR MIXING MASTERING ETC: Client asks for a request > I figure out what I can do timewise with Julianna, trying to stay realistic with calendar and true timing that the project will take and get their timeline > Tell them what I can do > add instruments and mix > send some rounds like v1, v2, v3 > master the project v1, v2 > send the deliverables.

FOR INSTALLS: put together a quote > email it over > get purchasing deposit from the client to initiate the project and start ordering products > install and coordinate labor > finish up project > follow up to get paid with invoice

FOR EVENTS: get an email request > send back a quote and put on calendar > coordinate who will take the event on, either me or another tech > fulfill that event > follow up with invoice post-event.


### Audio Production - Describe your typical session workflow:

Example: "Import stems → set up routing → rough mix → effects chains → automation..."

Get a rough project from client > set up whole project cleanly > add instruments > start mixing each instrument with plugins and chains, getting a rough general mix > mix additional FX and sounds for ear candy > send v1, v2 etc mix to client via dropbox for any adjustments or revisions > move onto mastering v1, v2 etc > send deliverables folder. all sent as dropbox links.
