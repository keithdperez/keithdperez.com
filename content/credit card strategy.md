## 4. Strategy Principles
1. **Attack one major balance at a time.** Focus fire = faster wins.
2. **Use cards only when autopay covers them.** No new revolving debt.
3. **Track totals every Friday.** Momentum comes from visibility.
4. **Use business revenue for business cards only.** Keeps taxes clean.
5. **Avoid balance transfers unless 0% promo + no fee.**
