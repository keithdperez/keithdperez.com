# Drawing from the reservoir

You don’t attract peace, clarity, or goodness by wishing for them. You draw them by living in alignment with truth—by staying connected to the deep reservoir within you.

Attraction isn’t about control. It’s not about visualizing outcomes or manifesting your dream life. That’s a distortion. The real thing is older than any trend. Scripture calls it sowing and reaping. It’s living from fullness instead of reacting from lack.

> *“Above all else, guard your heart, for everything you do flows from it.”* (Proverbs 4:23)  
> *“The good man brings good things out of the good stored up in his heart.”* (Luke 6:45)

What you feed is what flows. If you’re tuned to scarcity, you’ll move through life bracing for disappointment. If you’re rooted in truth, you’ll start to carry peace that isn’t shaken by what’s happening around you.

This isn’t about pretending everything is fine. It’s about choosing where to draw from. The world will always offer you a script of fear, urgency, and hustle. But the Spirit invites you to slow down, sink deeper, and live as if the kingdom is already within you—because it is.

So when life feels off, the question isn’t *what do I need to chase?*  
The question is *what am I drawing from?*
