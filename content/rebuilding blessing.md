# First Steps Toward Rebuilding Blessing

## 1. Humble Yourself
- **James 4:6** – God gives grace to the humble.  
- First prayer: *“Lord, I need You. I can’t fix this on my own.”*

## 2. Confess Honestly
- Admit where you’ve been wrong — to God first, then to Julianna.  
- Keep it simple: no excuses, no justifying.

## 3. Reconcile Relationally
- **1 Peter 3:7** – Mistreating your wife hinders prayers.  
- Ask her: *“What would help you feel cared for right now?”*  
- Don’t argue her answer. Just listen.

## 4. Obey in the Small Things
- **Deuteronomy 28** – Obedience brings blessing.  
- Start tiny: follow through on one thing today (meal, chore, text, prayer).

## 5. Root Yourself in Scripture
- **Psalm 1** – The blessed man delights in God’s Word.  
- Even if short, open the Bible daily. Anchor your steps in truth.

## 6. Accept Testing as Growth
- **John 15:2** – Pruning produces more fruit.  
- Don’t see setbacks as God’s absence, but His shaping.

---

### Daily Anchor
1. Pray humbly.  
2. Confess honestly.  
3. Take one obedient step.  
4. Reconcile where possible.  
5. Stay in the Word.  

*Blessing isn’t a switch to flip. It’s a path to walk.*
