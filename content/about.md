I'm Keith Perez—a husband, father, worship leader, and designer based in Illinois.

I run [then, create](https://thencreate.design), where I help churches and venues design and install audio/visual systems. I also produce and mix music, and lead worship at The Table.

This site is my digital garden—a place where I think out loud about faith, masculinity, marriage, and what it looks like to become the kind of man worth following.

---

## My Story

In my senior year of high school, I was drifting. Lying to my parents, sneaking around rules, doing my own thing on my own terms.

That summer—2008—my youth group went to Acquire the Fire. Before I left, my mom looked at me and said, *"Keith, don't be surprised when they tell you that you're going to face a choice—to follow God or follow destruction. You're at a fork in the road."*

Later that weekend, Ron Luce said almost the exact same thing, with the same hand gesture. That moment wrecked me. I knew I had to make a choice. That night, I chose Jesus.

Soon after, I enrolled in Rockford Master's Commission. That year was where I encountered God in a way I still carry to this day—raw worship, deep community, and a hunger for presence that shaped everything after.

---

## The Path Since

The story didn't go how I thought it would. There were plans for Europe, touring, building something big. Instead, God rooted me here.

After RMC, I became a youth leader, then transitioned into worship leading at The Life church in Lisle. When leadership changed, I stepped into the worship director role and helped shape a culture that didn't just play songs—we pursued the heart of God.

Along the way, God placed me in a family. I met Julianna on the worship team. We dated, got married in 2019, and now serve together at The Table.
