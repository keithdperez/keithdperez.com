## on sexual energy

That’s a deep and honest question — and it’s good that you’re not just asking “how do I make her want me,” but tying it to repentance, restraint, and rebuilding something real. That already puts you on a better path than most men who try to shortcut intimacy.

  

Let’s unpack this in layers:

  

  

  

  

1. Desire isn’t built through abstinence alone

  

  

Pulling back sexually can be right for a season — to detox your mind, stop the cycle of lust, and re-learn what actual connection feels like. But if you stay in a mode of “avoid all desire,” she won’t feel drawn to you. She’ll just feel distance.

  

What creates desire isn’t sex — it’s safety + polarity.

Safety comes from your repentance, consistency, and truth-telling.

Polarity comes from your energy — confidence, clarity, grounded masculine pursuit.

  

When you cut off sexual energy altogether, you risk also cutting off that polarity. The goal isn’t to suppress desire, it’s to reorder it. To let it serve love, not replace it.

  

  

  

  

2. Safety has to come first

  

  

If Julianna still doesn’t feel safe, her body literally won’t respond to desire. She has to feel:

  

- “He sees me.”
- “He tells the truth.”
- “He’s stable.”
- “He doesn’t take from me.”

  

  

So your job right now is to create emotional consistency — day after day, small trustworthy signals. Predictability. Transparency. Calm strength.

That’s the fertilizer desire grows in.

  

  

  

  

3. Then, reintroduce 

aliveness

  

  

Once she starts to feel safe, the next step isn’t jumping to sexual pursuit — it’s creating emotional electricity again.

That means:

  

- Doing things that make you come alive. She’ll feel that energy.
- Playful teasing, not sexual, just light and human.
- Holding her gaze a beat longer.
- Doing small acts of strength or leadership without announcing it.
- Complimenting her beauty in specific, grounded ways — not lustful, but intentional.

  

  

When she feels your restraint combined with warmth and confidence, she’ll feel attraction again — because it’s no longer coming from need, but from strength.

  

  

  

  

4. Separate 

purity

 from 

passivity

  

  

Some men, after falling into lust, swing to the opposite extreme — thinking purity means shutting down all sexual energy. But mature holiness isn’t suppression. It’s integration.

  

It’s saying: “I’m not ruled by desire, but I also don’t fear it. I channel it toward love, service, and intimacy.”

That’s what she needs to see — that your desire is for her, not for relief.
  

5. Questions to sit with
  

- When Julianna sees me now, does she feel my steadiness or my guilt?
- Am I trying to earn her trust, or embody it?
- Where in our daily life can I start being more alive — not for her reaction, but because it’s who I am?
- What would it look like to show desire without demanding response?
