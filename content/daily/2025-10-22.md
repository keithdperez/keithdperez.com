## This morning

I slept past my alarm, and the garbage truck drove by this morning at 6am. [[Julianna]] woke me up, And was frustrated because she feels the lack of care and she feels that she is doing and carrying the majority of life. She feels unsupported, and felt like I was digging my heels in by surfing, my phone and sitting on the chair while she was getting ready. This was her text message to me:

> Of course on a Wednesday when i have a 12hr day ahead, carrying the jobs of multiple people, preparing an event, meeting with people, and no breakfast….you would show me NO support…in fact you are abandoning me.
> You bring the harm upon our home and you have no remorse for it. 
> “Its too late.” How encouraging the words you choose to say to me.

## Later on

As I was working at [[Kellogg School of Management|Kellogg]], I had some down time and felt like I should call Julianna. I called her, and asked how she's doing — she was softened. She mentioned she just finished talking to a representative from Family Life (the host of Weekend to Remember), and had confirmed our roles as pastors. She said they prayed over our marriage.

Timing of these events align perfectly to me… **we must stay in the covering of the Lord.**

### How can this be practiced?

- Don't wait for the moment of conflict to act loving. *Preempt it.*
- Keep up with breakfast, coffee, small notes, quick texts and check-ins — it signals safety.
- Morning prayer — "Lord, cover my home today. Let my posture bring peace."
- Reflect on her deeper, spirit ual communication. *What is she trying to say to me?*
