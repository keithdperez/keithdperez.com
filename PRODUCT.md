# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Friends, church people and strangers who arrive at one essay through a shared link, usually on a phone. They came for one piece, and they should leave holding what it gives.

## Product Purpose
Keith D. Perez's writing on faith in ordinary life. Every published piece hands the reader something to use (a card, a song, a link), and a piece goes live only after Keith has reviewed it. See CLAUDE.md for the gates.

## Positioning
Curator, not authority: Keith points to Scripture, books and songs, and the reader can follow every source. The gift is the payoff, not an afterthought.

## Voice
