The heart is never neutral. It leans toward a source, and in time it takes the shape of what it trusts. This essay follows Jeremiah's image of a shrub in the desert and a tree planted beside water.

```bible
5 Cursed is the one who trusts in man...
6 That person will be like a bush in the wastelands.
8 They will be like a tree planted by the water.
Jeremiah 17:5–8
```

## The meaning

The image is not about effort alone. It is about the hidden source that sustains a life through drought.

> [!quote]
> The roots decide what the drought can take.

The stream is a picture of trust made visible in a life.
